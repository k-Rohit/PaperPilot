This approach addresses the high computational costs associated with rollout generation during RL post-training, particularly in the context of Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) [arXiv:2610.32881v1].

### Key Contributions of NgramRL:

1. **N-gram Cache Utilization**: NgramRL maintains a per-prompt n-gram cache built from prior rollouts of the same prompt. This cache allows the system to propose draft tokens based on previously generated outputs, effectively leveraging the temporal locality of prompts. As noted in the paper, across consecutive epochs, prior rollouts can cover 48-96% of later rollouts, indicating a significant overlap that NgramRL exploits to generate high-quality drafts for future epochs [arXiv:2610.32881v1].

2. **Model-Free Drafting**: The system operates as a model-free drafter, meaning it does not require additional model training or parameters. Instead, it uses the outputs from the policy's own rollouts to populate the n-gram cache, ensuring that the drafts remain aligned with the evolving policy [arXiv:2610.32881v1]. This self-updating mechanism helps maintain the relevance and accuracy of the drafts without the need for a persistent GPU-resident drafter state.
